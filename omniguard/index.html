    The rules behind it

    Built from the directives, not the blog posts.

    Directive (EU) 2019/2161

    Omnibus prior price

    Announce a price reduction and you must show the lowest price of the 30 days before it. Omniguard calculates it per variant and displays it.

    Directive 2011/83/EU · Art. 11a

    Online withdrawal

    Shoppers must be able to withdraw from a contract online as easily as they bought. The withdrawal block handles the flow and the confirmation.

    UK CMA guidance

    Honest "was" prices

    Reference prices should be ones you genuinely charged. The 30-day history and alerts help you show numbers you can back up.

    Questions

    Good to know.

    What does the Omnibus Directive actually require?

    When you announce a price reduction in the EU, you must also show the lowest price the product had during the 30 days before the reduction. Omniguard records your prices continuously, works out that number and displays it next to the discount.

    Will it slow down my store?

    No. The reference price is calculated in the background and saved on the product as a metafield. Your theme reads it like any other product field, with no extra requests when a page loads.

    Do I need a developer?

    No. Install from the Shopify App Store and add the Omniguard block in the theme editor. Older themes can use the app embed instead. No theme files are edited.

    Which products are tracked?

    Every active and unlisted product and variant, with no limit on catalogue size. Draft and archived prices were never offered to shoppers, so they don't count toward the 30-day low.

    I only sell partly in the EU. Can I hide it elsewhere?

    Yes. A switch in the block settings shows the label only to shoppers in the EU/EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland.

    Can I prove my price history if someone asks?

    Yes. Each snapshot is chained to the previous one with a SHA-256 hash, so a change to past records is detectable. The audit log shows the verification result and exports as CSV.

    Is this legal advice?

    No. Omniguard is a tool that helps you meet your pricing obligations. How the rules apply to your business is a question for your own legal adviser.
