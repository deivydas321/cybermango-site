Questions

What does the Omnibus Directive require?

When you announce a price reduction in the EU, you must also show the lowest price the product had in the 30 days before it. Omniguard records your prices, works out that number and displays it.

Do I need a developer?

No. Install the app and add the Omniguard block in your theme editor. No theme files are edited.

Will it slow down my store?

No. The price is calculated in the background and saved on the product, so your theme reads it like any other field.

Is this legal advice?

No. Omniguard is a tool that helps you meet your pricing obligations. How the rules apply to your business is a question for your own legal adviser.
